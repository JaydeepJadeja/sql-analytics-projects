# E-commerce Sales Exploration (SQL Case Study)

## Business Problem

The company wanted to identify underperforming inventory segments where high sales are not translating into strong profitability.

The goal was to analyze sales and profit data to find inefficiencies and improve decision-making around pricing, inventory, and product strategy.

---

## Dataset

Global Superstore Dataset

---

## Tools Used

* SQL (MySQL)
* Aggregation Functions
* GitHub

---

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales Analysis

* Total Sales
* Sales by Category
* Sales by Sub-Category
* Sales by Region

### 2. Profit Analysis

* Total Profit
* Profit by Category
* Profit by Sub-Category
* Loss-making Products

### 3. Profitability Analysis

* Profit Margin by Category

---

## Key Insights

* Technology generated the highest sales and profit.
* Furniture generated strong sales but had the lowest profit margin.
* Office Supplies generated lower sales but higher profit efficiency.
* Tables sub-category is causing losses to the business.
* Furniture is the least efficient category in terms of profit generation.

---

## Recommendations

* Investigate Furniture pricing and cost structure.
* Reduce losses in Tables sub-category.
* Scale Technology category due to strong performance.
* Analyze Office Supplies efficiency and replicate strategies.
